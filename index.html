Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a resume ATS-friendly?

An ATS-compliant resume uses standard section headings, clear typography, and a single-column hierarchy that applicant tracking algorithms can parse without dropped data.

Is my resume data stored or shared with employers?

No. The builder runs 100% locally in your web browser. Your employment history, contact information, and resumes are saved in local storage and never sent to any server.

How can I export my finished resume to PDF?

Click the Print or Save as PDF button. Your browser print dialog will open where you select Save as PDF formatted perfectly for standard US Letter or A4 paper.
